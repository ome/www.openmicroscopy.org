Organisers: Latin America Bioimaging

Location: Panama City, Panama

Workshop: OMERO Workshop Users' Track

Users' Track workshop: OMERO, a component of OME, is a software platform for image data management and analysis which will be presented in this workshop. Import, organisation, viewing, searching, annotation and publishing of imaging data with OMERO will be shown, including usage of the omero-biofilefinder plugin.

Further, the workshop will include the popular OMERO.figure application, now with ability to use S3-hosted OME-Zarr images.

Dates: 25-26 November 2026

Workshop: OMERO Workshop Developers' Track

Date: 27 November 2026

Developers' Track workshop: The workshop will concentrate on development of OMERO.web applications.
