Organisers: Latin America Bioimaging

Location: Panama City, Panama

Workshop: OMERO Workshop Users' Track

Dates: 25-26 November 2026

OMERO, a component of OME, is a software platform for image data management and analysis which will be presented in this workshop. Import, organisation, viewing, searching, annotation and publishing of imaging data with OMERO will be shown, including usage of the omero-biofilefinder plugin.

Further, the workshop will include the popular OMERO.figure application, now with ability to use S3-hosted OME-Zarr images.

Workshop: OMERO Workshop Developers' Track

Date: 27 November 2026

The workshop will concentrate on developing OMERO.web applications. We will guide participants through setting up their local development environment. Participants will then practice working with the OMERO.web source code or its plugins, and learn how to contribute their changes via GitHub.
