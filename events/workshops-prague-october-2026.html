Organisers: IOCB, Prague, Czech Republic

Location: IOCB, Prague, Czech Republic

Workshop: OMERO Workshop

Dates: 15 October 2026

OMERO, a component of OME, is a software platform for image data management and analysis which is presented in this workshop. Import, organisation, viewing, searching, annotation and publishing of imaging data with OMERO will be shown, also using the omero-biofilefinder plugin.

Further, the emphasis will be given on the popular OMERO.figure application, now with ability to use S3-based ome-zarr images.
