Organisers: CEITEC CELLIM Brno, Czech Republic

Location: CEITEC, Brno, Czech Republic

Workshop: OMERO Workshop

Date: 4 December 2026

OMERO, a component of OME, is a software platform for image data management and analysis which will be presented in this workshop. Import, organisation, viewing, searching, annotation and publishing of imaging data with OMERO will be shown, including usage of the omero-biofilefinder plugin.

Further, the workshop will include the popular OMERO.figure application, now with ability to use S3-hosted OME-Zarr images.
